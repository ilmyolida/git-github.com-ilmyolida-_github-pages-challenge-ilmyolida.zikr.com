PRIVACY POLICY FOR ZIKR - DAILY TASBIH & DHIKR

Last Updated: February 23, 2026

Safe Media Official operates the Zikr mobile application. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Data Privacy & Local Storage

Our primary principle is that your spiritual data belongs only to you. We believe in absolute privacy for your religious practices.

  • No Server Storage: All user-generated content, including custom zikrs, texts, audio files, and uploaded images, are stored strictly on the user’s local device storage.
  • Zero Data Access: Safe Media Official does not collect, access, or view your data. We do not maintain a central server database, and your information is never transmitted outside your phone.
  • No Registration: No account creation, email address, or any form of personal identification is required to use the app.

2. Background Location Access

The app requests ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION permissions to provide essential religious features.

  • Purpose: To calculate accurate prayer times (Salat) and determine the Qibla direction based on your current city and precise geographical coordinates.
  • Usage: Background access is necessary to trigger timely prayer notifications and provide accurate data even when the app is not actively running on your screen.
  • Policy: This location data is processed locally for astronomical calculations only. It is never shared, tracked, or uploaded to any server or third party.

3. Media and File Permissions

The app requests access to READ_MEDIA_IMAGES and READ_MEDIA_VIDEO to enhance user experience.

  • Purpose: To allow users to personalize their dhikr sessions by selecting their own images for backgrounds or uploading instructional audio and video content for learning purposes.
  • Security: The app only accesses the specific files you manually select using the system picker. It does not scan your entire gallery and never shares your media files with any external entities.

4. Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdMob for displaying banner advertisements to support the development of the app.

  • AdMob may use anonymous advertising IDs for ad delivery and frequency capping.
  • Important: No personal religious data, location history, or media information from our app is ever shared with AdMob or any third-party advertisers.

5. Disclosure and Consent

By using this app and granting the requested permissions, you consent to the local processing of your data as described in this policy. We provide prominent disclosures within the app interface before any sensitive permission is requested to ensure you are fully informed.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Since we do not collect any data on our servers, we remain compliant with global child protection regulations.
